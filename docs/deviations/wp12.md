## The rig (Blender side)

`Wolf_Armature` at the origin, rest pose = the WP1 standing pose, every bone rolled so its local X is +X (the WP11 convention, checked by `build_armature`).

| Bone | Head -> tail (Blender, m) | Parent | Parts (rigid, weight 1.0) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Hips` (root) | (0, -0.22, 0.45) -> (0, -0.06, 0.45) | - | haunches |
| `Spine` | -> (0, 0.32, 0.48) | `Hips` | torso, chest, belly |
| `Neck` | -> (0, 0.44, 0.53) | `Spine` | neck |
| `Head` | -> (0, 0.74, 0.52) | `Neck` | skull, muzzle, nose, jaw, ears, eyes |
| `Tail` | (0, -0.212, 0.483) -> (0, -0.388, 0.577) | `Hips` | tail |
| `FrontLeg_L/R`, `HindLeg_L/R` | (+/-0.105, 0.22 or -0.20, 0.40) -> same x/y, z 0 | `Hips` | upper box, lower box, paw |

Geometry, materials, names and the 312 triangles are unchanged from WP1; `finish_object` raises if any vertex has no bone. `OverheadAnchor` is parented to the armature object at y 0.925 and never bobs.

Clips, keyed every 2 frames at 60 fps, linear, last key = first, exported in `ACTIONS` mode like WP11:

| Clip | Length | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `idle` | 2.0 s (120 frames) | `Spine` pitches 0 to 1.2 degrees and back (breathing), `Neck` nods 1 degree, `Head` turns +/- 6 degrees and nods 1, `Tail` wags +/- 10 degrees once and lifts 2 degrees twice per loop |
| `trot` | 0.4 s (24 frames) | diagonal gait, duty factor 0.5: front-left with hind-right touch down at phase 0, front-right with hind-left at 0.5; legs +/- 28 degrees; body crouched 2.3 cm plus a bob of up to 2.3 cm at twice the step frequency; paws lift 6 cm at mid-swing; `Spine` rolls +/- 2 degrees, `Neck` lifts 3 degrees as the body sinks, `Head` sways -/+ 3 degrees against the `Tail`'s +/- 10 degree wag |

How the trot keeps a planted paw still: during its stance a paw moves back relative to the body at a constant rate (the leg angle is `asin` of a linear sweep, not a sine), and each leg slides along its own axis so the sole sits exactly at its lift height above z = 0 while the rigid leg tilts. Half of the height change is a body bob, half is the stance leg sliding up into the torso, where the 6 cm of leg hidden inside the body covers it.

Stride per cycle, measured on the posed rig by sampling the evaluated `FrontLeg_L` tail frame by frame (`SF_TROT` line): the paw sweeps 0.3756 m back over its 12-frame stance, which is 2 x 0.40 m x sin 28 degrees; the other diagonal pair carries the body over the other half-cycle, so the body covers 0.7512 m per cycle with no slide (the generator raises if the measurement and 4 L sin A disagree by more than 5 mm). Planted paw height at worst 1.3 mm (odd frames between keys). `Enemy3D.TROT_REFERENCE_SPEED` = 0.7512 m / 0.4 s = 1.878 m/s, so the wolf's 3.6 m/s chase plays the clip at 1.92x, 4.8 cycles per second.

Previews: `wolf.png` at rest as before; `wolf_trot.png` at frame 3 (phase 0.125: the front-left pair planted, the other pair lifting) from a 70 degree yaw, as the WP11 walk sheets.

## How the importer laid it out

    Wolf (Node3D)
      Wolf_Armature (Node3D)
        Skeleton3D (9 bones)
          Wolf_Body (MeshInstance3D, skinned)
        OverheadAnchor
      AnimationPlayer (idle 2.00 s, trot 0.40 s, both LOOP_LINEAR, 13 tracks each)

`wolf_3d.tscn` instances the glb under `Model/WolfModel` and needed no change: `Enemy3D` finds meshes and the AnimationPlayer recursively, and uses the scene's own `OverheadAnchor` (y 0.95), not the glb's.

## How the enemy drives the clips

Every physics frame `Enemy3D._update_locomotion_animation` plays `trot` while `is_moving()` and the ground speed (`velocity` on x/z) is above 0.2 m/s, with `speed_scale = speed / TROT_REFERENCE_SPEED` clamped to 0.25..3.0, else `idle`, cross-fading 0.15 s. `get_locomotion_state()` and `get_animation_player()` report it. `_on_died` pauses the player and clears the state, so the topple and collapse run on a frozen pose. Without an AnimationPlayer under `Model` (the generic box) nothing runs and the state stays empty. The lunge, recoil and topple still tween `Model` and the tints are overlays, so they play on top.

## Deviations and decisions

| # | Area | Deviation | Why | Action for the lead |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Leg bones | One bone per leg (`FrontLeg_L/R`, `HindLeg_L/R`), no upper/lower split. The swing lift and the stance compensation are a slide of the leg along its own axis into the torso instead of a knee bend. | The brief's condition: the WP1 leg is an upper box, a lower box and a paw with no joint box between them, so a bent lower leg would open a visible gap. | None, unless a later model adds a hock box. |
| 2 | Leg parents | All four legs hang from `Hips`, the front legs too, rather than from `Spine`. | The idle breathing and the trot roll move `Spine`; with the front legs under it, a planted front paw would lift and slide by a few millimetres every breath. | None. |
| 3 | Trot cadence | At 3.6 m/s the wolf takes 4.8 strides a second (1.92x playback). | The WP1 legs are 0.40 m from joint to sole, so +/- 28 degrees sweeps 0.75 m per cycle. A longer stride at this leg length needs a suspension phase (a flying trot or a gallop), which the brief did not ask for. | Accept, or ask for a gallop clip for the full-speed chase. |
| 4 | Crouch while trotting | The body sits 2.3 cm lower while trotting and bobs up to 2.3 cm more; the cross-fade eases it in. | Taking the whole 4.7 cm of height change as bob looked jittery at almost 10 bobs a second; half goes into the legs instead. `TROT_BOB_SHARE` in the generator sets the split. | None. |
| 5 | Clip process mode | `Enemy3D` switches the wolf's AnimationPlayer to `ANIMATION_CALLBACK_MODE_PROCESS_PHYSICS`. The player's WP11 code keeps the idle default. | The clip then advances in the same steps as the body. At 3.6 m/s a one-frame phase difference between pose and body is 6 cm of apparent paw slip. | Consider the same for `SoulBodies3D` if the walk ever shows jitter. |
| 6 | Trot grace | The 0.12 s grace keeps `trot` even when `is_moving()` drops for a frame, unlike the player's rule. | The chase re-aims whenever the agent finishes its path, which leaves one frame with no path and would restart the cross-fade. A stopping wolf therefore trots in place for at most 0.12 s. | None. |
| 7 | Death | `_on_died` pauses the clip (`pause()`, pose kept) rather than `stop()`. | `pause()` keeps the current pose and playback position by definition, while `stop()` resets the position; a frozen mid-stride pose topples naturally. | None. |
| 8 | Glb node paths | `Wolf_Body` moved from the glb root to `Wolf/Wolf_Armature/Skeleton3D/Wolf_Body`, `OverheadAnchor` from under `Wolf_Body` to `Wolf/Wolf_Armature/OverheadAnchor`. | The importer's layout for a skinned mesh, as in WP11. The regenerated prop previews differed only in Blender's PNG `tEXt Date` stamp, so the committed ones were kept; the prop `.import` files are unchanged. | The brief: geometry and names must not change. | None. |
