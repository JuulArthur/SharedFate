## The rig (Blender side)

One armature `<Name>_Armature` per character, at the origin, rest pose = the WP1 standing pose. Every bone is rolled so its local X is the character's right (+X): a rotation about local X is a forward/up pitch for every bone (the generator checks this). Heads and tails follow the part boxes: hips at the belt, knees and elbows at the joint boxes, shoulders at the pauldron or shoulder box.

| Character | Bones |
| --- | --- |
| Knight (12) | `Hips`, `Spine`, `Head`, `UpperArm_L/R`, `LowerArm_L/R`, `UpperLeg_L/R`, `LowerLeg_L/R`, `Cape` |
| Rogue (11) | `Hips`, `Spine`, `Head`, `UpperArm_L/R`, `LowerArm_L/R`, `UpperLeg_L/R`, `LowerLeg_L/R` |
| Mage (8) | `Hips`, `Spine`, `Head`, `UpperArm_L/R`, `LowerArm_L/R`, `Robe` |

Skinning is rigid: `sf_assets_common.skin_begin(bm)` / `skin_bone(name)` record the owning bone of every primitive while the body bmesh is built, and `finish_object` turns the record into one vertex group per bone, weight 1.0 (it raises if a vertex has no bone). Head parts (neck, helm, hood, hair, eyes) go to `Head`; torso, belt, buckle, straps, mantle, scarf and sash to `Spine`; pelvis / hips box to `Hips`; pauldron, trim, shoulder box and upper arm (mage: sleeve) to `UpperArm`; elbow, forearm, bracer, cuff, gauntlet and hand to `LowerArm`; cuisse / thigh to `UpperLeg`; knee, greave / shin, boot and toe to `LowerLeg`; the knight's cape to `Cape`; the mage's skirt, hem and feet to `Robe`. The body stays one `<Name>_Body` object with an Armature modifier, object-parented to the armature. `HandPoint` and the right-hand weapon are bone-parented to `LowerArm_R`, the rogue's `Dagger_L` to `LowerArm_L`, `OverheadAnchor` to the armature object (it never bobs, so the bars stay put).

Clips, keyed every 2 frames at 60 fps with linear interpolation, the last key repeating the first; plain actions, no NLA; exported in `ACTIONS` mode with `export_anim_single_armature` (one armature per file):

| Clip | Length | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `idle` | 2.0 s (120 frames) | spine sinks 2 cm and back (breathing), upper arms sway 3 degrees, forearms 4 +/- 2 degrees, head nods 1.5 degrees; cape 0 to 3 degrees back; robe rolls 1 degree |
| `walk` | 1.0 s (60 frames), one stride for 3.5 m/s | legs +/- 30 degrees at the hips (right leg forward at 0.25 s), knee straight through the stance and bending from heel-off to 59 degrees in the swing; arms counter-swing +/- 25 degrees (mage 10 right with the staff, 18 left), elbows 8 to 20 degrees; hips drop up to 3 cm at twice the stride frequency and twist 3 degrees; torso leans 5 degrees and counter-twists; head levels; cape trails 12 to 20 degrees; mage robe trails 1 to 5 degrees, twists 5 and rolls 4 degrees instead of stepping |

Measured: knight 988 triangles in Godot with the solidified cape (832 without), rogue 720, mage 648; heights 1.995 / 1.890 / 1.925 m; `HandPoint` (0.335, 0.86, -0.08) / (0.265, 0.83, -0.06) / (0.245, 0.92, -0.06), `OverheadAnchor` 2.245 / 2.140 / 2.140 m, all unchanged from WP1. At mid-stride (0.25 s) the knight's hand is 0.21 m behind its rest position. Previews: `<name>.png` at rest as before, `<name>_walk.png` at 0.25 s of the walk.

## How the player drives the clips

- `SoulBodies3D` finds each body's `Skeleton3D` and `AnimationPlayer` by class, enforces `LOOP_LINEAR` on `idle` and `walk` (the import settings already set it), and exposes `get_animation_player()`, `get_skeleton()` and `get_live_hand_transform()`. Rest measurements (`get_hand_transform`, the weapon fit, the anchor height) walk the local transform chain with a bone attachment replaced by its bone's global rest, so they no longer depend on the attachment having been posed.
- Every physics frame `Player3D._update_locomotion_animation` plays `walk` while `is_moving()` and the ground speed (`velocity` on x/z) is above 0.2 m/s, with `speed_scale = speed / 3.5` (clamped 0.25 to 1.5), else `idle`, cross-fading 0.15 s. A walk survives 0.12 s without speed while the actor still reports moving, so an avoidance frame never flickers it to idle. `get_locomotion_state()` reports the clip.
- A shift hands the clip over: `show_soul` starts the new body on the old body's clip, position and speed scale (the test checks the phase within 0.05 s).
- The player's `HandPoint` follows the animated hand: `SoulBodies3D` re-emits `active_pose_updated` on the active skeleton's `skeleton_updated`, on its own local transform change (the lunge, recoil and squash tweens on `Model` run after the skeleton update in a frame) and on each step of the shift punch (now a method tween), and `Player3D._follow_body_hand` sets `HandPoint.transform = get_live_hand_transform()` (orthonormalised, so the `Model:scale` squash never squashes the weapon).

## What a wolf trot would need

- `generate_wolf.py`: an armature (`Hips`, `Spine`, `Neck`, `Head`, `Tail`, and `UpperLeg`/`LowerLeg` for the four legs, front and hind), rigid vertex groups by part as here, and `idle` plus `trot` actions: diagonal pairs in phase (left fore with right hind), about a 0.5 s cycle at the wolf's 3.6 m/s, a small body bob at twice the step frequency, tail and head counter-sway. Export with `animations=True` and loop the clips in `wolf.glb.import`. `OverheadAnchor` stays on the armature object so the 0.925 m bar height holds. A `bite` clip timed to the wind-up and strike durations would replace part of the procedural lunge.
- `enemy_3d.gd`: the same velocity rule as the player (trot above 0.2 m/s, `speed_scale = speed / 3.6`), a hand-over to `idle` when stopped or rooted, and no locomotion clip during the topple. It already poses, tints and topples only nodes under `Model` from the pose captured in `_ready`, and `HitFlash3D` and the hover overlay work on skinned meshes, so the rest of the script needs no change. The `AnimationPlayer` must be found recursively under `Model`.
- Tests: `enemy_test.gd` and the arena smoke would assert the clips like the player's.
