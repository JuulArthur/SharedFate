# WP13 "Detection and ambush": decisions and deviations

Package: WP13, branch `feat/3d-wp13`, based on `dcf440c`. Written 2026-09-25.
Contract: `docs/3d-port-contracts.md`, sections 2, 5, 7, 9, 11, 12. The lead folds accepted rows into the contract's deviations table.
Request: a visible range in which enemies spot the player outside combat, a larger radius for enemies joining a fight that is starting, and the player's attacks usable outside combat so that a hit (or being spotted first) starts the fight.

Files created: `scenes/3d/tests/detection_test.tscn`, `scripts/3d/tests/detection_test.gd` (plus `.uid`), this file.
Files edited: `scripts/3d/main_3d.gd`, `scripts/3d/enemy_3d.gd`, `scripts/3d/player_3d.gd` (attack availability outside combat and the exploration spell cooldown only), `scenes/3d/wolf_3d.tscn`, `scenes/3d/enemy_3d.tscn`, `scripts/3d/tests/arena_integration_smoke.gd`, `enemy_test.gd`, `approach_test.gd`, `main3d_smoke.gd`, `docs/3d-arena.md`.
No 2D file, shared autoload or model was touched. `scenes/3d/arena.tscn` did not need to change: the spawn at (-9, 0, 0) is 7.5 m from the nearest wolf ring edge plus radius (8.6 m to Enemy_02 and Enemy_03, 9 m to Enemy_01, all outside 5 m).

## The rules as the player experiences them

1. Every enemy shows a faint dashed ring on the ground in exploration at its detection range. It turns red when the player is within 1.5 m of the edge. Rings are gone in combat, once an enemy is alerted, while it dies, and for an unaware enemy (range 0).
2. Inside the ring with a clear line of sight the enemy spots the player: turn combat starts at once with `COMBAT!`, the player's turn first, everyone within 12 m of the player engaged. Newcomers still join at each enemy turn.
3. In exploration the action row offers Melee, Throw (rogue) and the two spells (mage). A landed hit on an enemy outside combat starts it as an ambush: `AMBUSH!`, the hit resolved first, the struck enemy and everyone within 12 m engaged, the enemies' turn first, then the player's. A refused, out-of-range or missed attack starts nothing.
