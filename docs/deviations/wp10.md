# WP10 "Approach spacing": diagnosis and deviations

Package: WP10, branch `feat/3d-wp10`, based on `780b239`. Written 2026-09-25.
Contract: `docs/3d-port-contracts.md`, sections 2, 3, 5.1, 5.2, 5.3, 9, 11, 12.
Report: when the player and a wolf close in to attack, they run into each other and bump each other around.

Files created: `scenes/3d/tests/approach_test.tscn`, `scripts/3d/tests/approach_test.gd` (plus `.uid`), this file.
Files edited: `scripts/3d/actor_3d_base.gd`, `scripts/3d/enemy_3d.gd`, `scripts/3d/player_3d.gd` (pursuit, approach and range code only), `scripts/3d/main_3d.gd` (engage and enemy-turn approach), `scenes/3d/player_3d.tscn` (`NavigationAgent3D` only), `scenes/3d/enemy_3d.tscn`, `scenes/3d/wolf_3d.tscn` (agent and `attack_range`).
No 2D file, model, visual function or `Model` / `HandPoint` subtree was touched. No existing test assertion changed.

## 1. Diagnosis (measured before any code change)

`approach_test.tscn` puts the real `player_3d.tscn` and one `wolf_3d.tscn` on a 40 x 40 m floor with a runtime navmesh and logs every physics frame: the ground distance between the two origins, the gap between the two collision surfaces along the line of centres, both velocity magnitudes, and the "bump", a displacement the body did not command (any displacement of a body that intends to stand, or the part beyond its own step for a body that intends to walk). Three phases: A the wolf aggroes on a standing player; B the player is sent to attack a wolf that is also approaching; C turn mode, the coordinator's engage (approach point, navmesh path, trim, attack) and then the enemy's turn.

| Phase | Closest approach | Rest distance (surface gap) | Bumps | Attacks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A wolf to standing player | 0.90 m (surfaces overlap 0.24 m) | 0.90 m (-0.10 m) | player pushed 33 frames, up to 0.028 m/frame (1.7 m/s), 0.80 m in total, while standing; wolf 16 frames, 0.035 m/frame, 0.56 m after it had halted | bite lands |
| B both approach | 0.94 m (-0.18 m) | 0.94 m (-0.06 m) | player 3 frames up to 0.051 m/frame beyond its own step (about 6.6 m/s); wolf 8 frames, 0.30 m | both land |
| C turn mode | 1.13 m (0.13 m) | 1.13 m | none above 0.02 m | player engage stops at 1.32 m, `try_attack` refused (reach 1.20); wolf stops at 1.13 m, refused (reach 1.10) |

What the numbers say:

1. **The bodies never collide physically.** Both actors have `collision_mask = 9` (layers 1 and 4); layer 2 is not in it, so an actor's `move_and_slide` ignores the other actor. Section 3's "actor-actor blocking" and WP3c's "actors now block each other" were never true; the wolf's box and the player's capsule overlapped by up to 0.24 m without any physics response. The 2D game did not collide either (contract intent aside), so this is not changed here; it is recorded for the lead (section 3 should say mask 9 = ground + props, no actor-actor blocking).
2. **The pushing is the avoidance simulation.** `avoidance_enabled` is on for both agents. RVO/ORCA splits every dodge between the two agents, so a standing player is shoved aside by an approaching wolf (0.80 m in phase A), and the NavigationServer keeps the last submitted velocity and emits `velocity_computed` every physics frame, so a wolf that had halted inside its reach kept sliding into the player (0.56 m), because `ActorBase3D._on_velocity_computed` moved the body on every callback. The two bodies settle at 0.90 m, which is the sum of the two avoidance radii (0.4 + 0.5), not of the collision shapes. The RVO answer is also not limited to the walking speed (agent `max_speed` default 10 m/s), which is the 6.6 m/s sideways fling in phase B.
3. **The approach goals lie inside the other body.** Player: `get_melee_range() - attack_approach_buffer` = 1.2 - 0.4 = 0.8 m; wolf chase: 1.1 - 0.5 = 0.6 m; coordinator enemy turn: `max(0.2, 1.1 - 0.4)` = 0.7 m. Contact between the capsule (r 0.35) and the wolf box (half depth 0.65) is at 1.0 m.
4. **`target_desired_distance` 0.4 m defeats the buffer in turn mode.** The turn move ends up to 0.4 m short of the approach point, so with the 0.4 m buffer the mover stops at about `reach + 0.1`, and both `try_attack` calls are refused after a full engage ("Out of reach" in play). The buffers had been widened in WP3c/WP4 to compensate for this, which is what put the goals inside the bodies.
5. The 1 s default `time_horizon_agents` makes two agents closing head-on brake metres early (phase B took 2.6 s for 5 m once the pushing was gone; the wolf ran at 2.1 m/s instead of 3.6 in phase A).

Attack reach was a limiting factor only for the wolf: its box reaches 0.65 m ahead of its origin, so a 1.1 m reach leaves 0.1 m past the player's surface, less than the gap plus the arrival slack.

## 2. What changed and why

| # | File | Change | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `actor_3d_base.gd` | `_on_velocity_computed` only moves the body on a frame `_step_navigation` asked the simulation for a velocity (`_avoidance_step_requested`), and limits the answer to `move_speed`. A halt or arrival submits a zero velocity once (`_submit_avoidance_velocity`). | Ends the phantom sliding after a halt and the shoving of a standing body; a body never moves faster than it walks. |
| 2 | `actor_3d_base.gd` | New `hold_position()`: stand still, park the agent (`is_moving()` false, stuck timer reset), tell avoidance the body stays put; unlike `stop_movement_immediately` it has no subclass side effects. | The chase and the pursuit stop chasing once within reach instead of re-issuing a goal every refresh. As a side effect the player's stuck detector no longer fires after 1 s of standing in reach, which used to clear the attack target through `stop_movement_immediately`. |
| 3 | `actor_3d_base.gd` | One approach rule, `get_attack_approach_distance(target, reach, reach_margin)`: `clamp(reach - margin, own radius + target radius + APPROACH_SURFACE_GAP_M (0.1), reach - target_desired_distance)`, with `get_collision_radius()` / static `collision_radius_of()` (capsule, sphere, cylinder radius; box half extent; 0.4 m fallback). `APPROACH_REACH_MARGIN_M` = 0.2. | Keeps the surfaces 0.1 m apart and still guarantees the attack after arrival. When both cannot hold (a dagger, 0.96 m, against the 1.3 m wolf box) reach wins and the surfaces may touch. Player vs wolf: 1.1 m; wolf vs player: 1.1 m. |
| 4 | `enemy_3d.gd` | `_halt()` is `hold_position()`; the approach is the base rule (`APPROACH_BUFFER_M` 0.5 and `MIN_APPROACH_DISTANCE_M` are gone); within reach the chase is parked and only re-aimed when the target steps out of reach (refresh interval, or at once when parked); `_attack_precheck` uses the base 0.05 m tolerance (WP3c deviation 5 compared strictly). | Cause 2 to 4. |
| 5 | `player_3d.gd` | `_pursue_attack_target` holds position within reach and re-aims only when out of reach; `get_preferred_attack_approach_distance(target = null)` (additive optional parameter, default the current attack target) uses the base rule with the weapon reach and `attack_approach_buffer` as the margin; `MIN_APPROACH_DISTANCE_M` removed. | Cause 2 to 4. |
| 6 | `main_3d.gd` | New static `approach_distance_for(mover, target, reach)`: the mover's `get_attack_approach_distance` when it has one, else its `get_preferred_attack_approach_distance()`, else the old `max(0.2, reach - 0.4)` (stubs). Used by `_request_player_turn_engage_enemy` and `_request_enemy_turn_move_by_distance`. `_request_player_turn_attack` and `_player_can_attack_enemy_now` allow `ATTACK_RANGE_TOLERANCE_M` 0.05 as the actors do. | Turn combat stops at the same spacing as the realtime chase; an approach that ends exactly on the reach is not refused by rounding. |
| 7 | `player_3d.tscn`, `enemy_3d.tscn`, `wolf_3d.tscn` (`NavigationAgent3D`) | `target_desired_distance` 0.4 to 0.1 m; `max_speed` = the actor's `move_speed` (3.5 / 3.0 / 3.6); `time_horizon_agents` 1.0 to 0.25 s. | The walk ends where it is told (the rule assumes at most 0.1 m of slack); RVO cannot answer faster than the walk; two agents closing head-on no longer brake metres early. |
| 8 | `wolf_3d.tscn`, `enemy_3d.tscn` | `attack_range` 1.1 / 1.2 to **1.3 m**. | Reach is measured from the body origin, and both boxes extend 0.65 m ahead of it (the wolf model's nose is 0.75 m ahead). With a 1.0 m contact distance, 0.1 m gap and 0.1 m arrival slack the bite needs at least 1.2 m; 1.3 leaves 0.1 m of margin. The player's 1.2 m (weapon, contract) is unchanged: contact 1.0 + 0.1 + 0.1 = 1.2 fits exactly and the 0.05 m tolerance covers rounding. |

Not changed, and why: the collision masks (adding layer 2 would make actors block each other for the first time, a gameplay change the contract text claims but the 2D game never had; spacing is now guaranteed by the approach rule and the halt, so pushing cannot happen without it); the wolf collision box (0.9 x 1.3 m against a 0.31 x 1.15 m model; a tighter box would ease the rule further but changes click picking and blocked cells, left for a later package); the player's weapon reach.

## 4. Tests

- `approach_test.tscn` fails when any frame shows a bump above 0.02 m, or when a phase ends with the surfaces less than 0.1 m apart, or when a turn-mode attack is refused after the engage move. It runs the coordinator's static `approach_distance_for` and mirrors `_compute_approach_world_point`, `_build_world_path_from_navigation` and `_point_on_path_at_distance` for the path and trim (the coordinator itself cannot be in the scene: it starts combat by proximity, which the realtime phases must avoid). Phase C wires the wolf's target before switching to turn mode, as `_start_turn_based_combat` does after `_wire_enemy_ai_targets`; wiring it afterwards routes the wolf at once and it drifts 0.2 m during the player's move.
- No assertion in `enemy_test.gd`, `player_test.gd`, `arena_integration_smoke.gd` or `main3d_smoke.gd` needed a new number. Observed changes: the wolf now closes to 1.26 m (was 0.60 to 0.72 m); the smoke's 10 m request now walks 5.95 m of its 6 m budget (was 5.60 m, the old 0.4 m arrival slack); the stub arena's turn move still ends 0.86 m from the stub enemy (stubs use the fallback rule).

## 5. Contract notes for the lead

- Section 3: actor bodies with mask 9 do not collide with layer 2; drop "actor-actor blocking" or add layer 2 deliberately. WP3c deviation 9 ("actors now block each other", avoidance "to compensate") is wrong for the same reason.
- Section 5.1: add `hold_position()`, `get_attack_approach_distance()`, `get_collision_radius()` and the static `collision_radius_of()`; `velocity_computed` is applied only on frames the body asked to move.
- Section 5.2: `get_preferred_attack_approach_distance(target: Node3D = null)`; `attack_approach_buffer` is now the reach margin, floored by the spacing rule.
- Section 5.3: wolf reach 1.3 m (base enemy 1.3 m); the chase aims by the base rule, not "0.5 m short of `attack_range`".
- Section 2: the enemy turn approach is no longer `max(0.2, attack_range - 0.4)` for real actors; that formula survives only as the stub fallback in `main_3d.gd`.
- Section 9 / WP4 deviation 3: `target_desired_distance` is 0.1 m on all three actor scenes.
